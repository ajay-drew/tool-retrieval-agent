# Refund policy

## Refund windows
Physical products can be refunded within 30 days of delivery. Digital products (courses, downloads, licences) can be refunded within 14 days of purchase, as long as less than 20% of the content was accessed. Services that were already delivered are not refunded; offer a credit instead.

## Who approves refunds
Anyone on the team can issue a refund up to $500. A refund over $500 needs the finance lead's approval before it is issued. Note the approver's name in the refund note.

## Partial refunds
Partial refunds are fine for damaged items or late deliveries. Always refund the original payment (the capture), never send a separate payout as a refund.
