# Subscription plans

## Plans
Basic is $9 a month for 1 seat and standard-definition streaming. Pro is $29 a month and includes 5 seats, HD streaming and priority support. Both are billed monthly through PayPal subscriptions.

## Suspensions
Suspend a subscription when a payment fails twice. If it is still suspended after 60 days, cancel it and send the customer a win-back email.

## Upgrades
Customers can move between plans at any time; the new price applies from the next billing cycle.
