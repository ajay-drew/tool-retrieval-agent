# Invoicing guide

## Numbering
Invoice numbers use the prefix NW- followed by a four-digit sequence, for example NW-0142. Never reuse a number, even for a cancelled invoice.

## Payment terms
Our standard payment term is net 30: payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. Retainer clients are invoiced on the 1st of the month.

## Currency
US customers are invoiced in USD. EU customers are invoiced in EUR, and UK customers in GBP.

## Changing or cancelling
A draft can be edited or deleted freely. A sent invoice can be cancelled while it is unpaid. Once an invoice has been paid it can't be cancelled; issue a refund instead.
