# Dispute playbook

## First response
Reply to every new dispute within 48 hours, even if it's only to say we're looking into it. Check the order, the shipment and any messages from the buyer before replying.

## Evidence by dispute type
For item-not-received disputes, send the carrier tracking number and proof of delivery. For not-as-described disputes, send the product listing, photos and any messages where the buyer agreed to the item's condition. For unauthorised-payment disputes, send the IP address, device and shipping address match.

## Settling with an offer
If the buyer has a fair point, offer a partial refund to settle. The most we offer to settle is 50% of the disputed amount; above that, accept the claim instead.

## Escalating to a claim
Escalate a dispute to a claim (so PayPal decides) only when the disputed amount is over $200 and we have tracking or delivery proof. Below $200 it's cheaper to settle or accept.
